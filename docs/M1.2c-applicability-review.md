## Interpretation

**P** means behavior is stated in the quoted documentation, but applicability to the exact endpoint depends on the documented snapshot policy. It is not a source quotation naming that exact patch release. **E+P** means an explicit historical/transition statement plus policy mapping to the two selected endpoints; family-level text does not prove every intermediate or future version.

A supported assertion does not validate every sentence in its unit, prove the API at another endpoint invalid, or establish runtime correctness. Deprecation is not removal. Historical V1 explanations in S2 remain S2 documents describing V1. Context dependencies are identified below; nothing is added to searchable evidence. Optional context additions in corrections are proposals only.
