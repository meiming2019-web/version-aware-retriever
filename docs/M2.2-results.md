## Limits and interpretation

The aggregate numbers expose different tradeoffs, not an overall winner.
BM25 places a positive first for each answerable question but retrieves fewer
distinct positives by rank five. Dense covers the approved positives sooner
below rank one, while surfacing the single approved wrong-version item earlier.
Both return results without a corpus-supported answer for the one unsupported
query. Neither method is version-aware yet.

Only **one approved hard-negative behavior family** is represented: validator
TypeError wrapping/propagation. The broader wrong-version-negative coverage target
is not satisfied. Four exposed development questions cannot establish broad
generalization, complete the full benchmark, or substitute for held-out evaluation.
The human batch review followed prior ranking exposure; it is not blind or an
independent second annotation. No source examples were executed, no async runtime
capability was tested, and no answer generation or citation assessment was added.
Approved labels, Policy B and retrieval configurations were not altered after
seeing scores; BM25 was not rerun and Dense was not re-encoded.
